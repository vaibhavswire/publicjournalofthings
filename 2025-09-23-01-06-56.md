Hermitian topology refers to the study of topological properties in quantum systems described by Hermitian Hamiltonians, which are central to quantum mechanics and conserve probability, leading to real eigenvalues and real-valued observables. However, recent research explores the connections and distinctions between this established Hermitian topology and the emerging field of non-Hermitian topology, which arises in systems with energy loss or gain
